# RailRecover product vision

RailRecover is an independent, evidence-led coordination layer for railway lost-property response. Its purpose is to make the next accountable action visible to passengers and authorised operational teams without claiming to replace an operator's inventory, reporting or custody system.

## Product promise

One report should produce a clear, bounded set of responsible checks. Every response should identify who acted, what was checked, when it happened and what remains unknown.

## Who it serves

- passengers who need a factual next step after losing an important item;
- station, onboard, cleaning and lost-property teams who need a shared case view;
- case coordinators who manage overdue tasks, escalations and verification;
- pilot stakeholders who need measurable workflow evidence before integration.

## Principles

1. Evidence before inference.
2. A service number is not a physical train identity.
3. A negative check is local and time-bound, not proof of permanent loss.
4. A potential match is not a recovery.
5. Human verification precedes ownership and release.
6. Synthetic demo records are visibly separated from connected operational data.

The current repository implements a local simulation of the shared-response workflow. Authentication, operator integrations and production-grade privacy controls remain future work and are documented as such.
